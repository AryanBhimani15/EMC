Business Capabilities

Functional Building Blocks

Designed for commercial, operational and financial clarity across the cargo lifecycle.

ULD Inventory

Maintain a current view of available containers and pallets by station. Improve planning with accurate equipment inventory data.

ULD Tracking

Track ULD location, status and movement history across the network. Reduce blind spots around valuable assets.

ULD Allocation

Assign ULDs to flights and movements based on demand and availability. Improve equipment utilization across operations.

ULD Rotation

Plan circulation patterns to keep equipment in the right places. Support balanced network flows and reduced shortages.

ULD Maintenance

Schedule checks and maintenance events for serviceable equipment. Protect safety and availability with disciplined planning.

Damage Management

Record damage events, accountability and repair actions systematically. Lower losses and improve asset lifecycle control.
