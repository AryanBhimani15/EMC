EMC | Ease My Cargo

EMC (Ease My Cargo) builds the EMC Aviation Commerce Platform and its Airline Cargo Suite — a configurable, cloud-native platform for the air cargo industry.

What we do

Connected Aviation Commerce

EMC Airline Cargo Suite digitizes and orchestrates the entire air cargo lifecycle — from commercial engagement and capacity planning through operational execution, regulatory compliance and financial settlement.

The suite is built on the EMC Digital Platform, a low-code foundation that lets airlines and their partners configure screens, workflows and business rules without extensive software development.
